# T-20 Review

T-20 is the final candidate review at the frozen target. Snapshot 3 is the T-20 projection observation. A later observation or favorable report cannot replace a missed Snapshot 3.

## Procedure

1. Bind the candidate to the same run, canonical identity, event, and prior M6 audit. Review only candidates in M8's frozen ceiling pool.
2. Check the authoritative M5 ledger for Snapshot 3, its target time, provenance, and accepted or missed receipt. Do not backfill, retime, reconstruct, or substitute a supporting-source report for a projection snapshot.
3. Review current, attributable evidence for projection movement, availability, role, teammate/opponent changes, and relevant game conditions. Preserve source and timestamp gaps. Use only authorized sources and access methods; do not retrieve from prohibited or unavailable live sources.
4. Use `fifth_pick.t20_review.review_t20()` and preserve its readiness, reason codes, and audit. Do not create a manual freshness threshold or infer that favorable news cures a missing required check.
5. Return only `STILL STRONG`, `DOWNGRADED`, or `REMOVE RECOMMENDED`, with the recorded reasons and gaps. If Snapshot 3 or another required check is missing, preserve the blocked/removal outcome produced by M7.
6. Leave any removal and resulting vacancy unchanged. Do not promote a candidate outside the frozen pool or treat the M7 conclusion as Joseph's final decision.

## Boundaries

- Snapshot 3 remains observation 3; a late or missed snapshot is not rewritten as on time.
- A supporting-source update is not a projection-board observation.
- T-20 does not expand authorization, rerank the slate, fill a slot, or approve a final selection.
